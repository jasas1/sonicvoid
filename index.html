The engineering behind the lore. Drag the values; the curves are computed from datasheet models, not drawn.

Signal path

One loop, source to headphones. Amber dots are the hot phase, steel dots the cold phase; inside each tube, electrons leave the filament and land on the plate. Watch the two 2A3 plate currents: both stay above zero for the whole cycle. That is class A. The transformer takes their difference, which is what push-pull means. Plate curves and load line

2A3 Koren model (μ 4.2). The hyperbola is the 15 W plate limit; the line is the load each tube sees. Push the bias or the load and watch the swing.

Rectifier sag

A rectifier is a resistor that changes with current. The drop at rated current comes from each datasheet; the curve follows the three-halves law. More drop, more sag, softer dynamics.

Output transformer

Turns ratio squared sets the reflected load; primary inductance sets the bass corner; leakage inductance sets the treble corner. Change the headphones and the load line on the curves above moves.
