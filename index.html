Signal path

One loop, source to headphones. Amber dots are the hot phase, steel dots the cold phase; inside each tube, electrons leave the filament and land on the plate. Watch the two 2A3 plate currents: both stay above zero for the whole cycle. That is class A. The transformer takes their difference, which is what push-pull means. Hover, tap or tab to a stage to read what it does. Representative topology, not Woo's schematic.

Plate curves and load line

2A3 Koren model (μ 4.2). The hyperbola is the 15 W plate limit; the line is the load each tube sees. Push the bias or the load and watch the swing.

Driver stage

The 6C45 in front of each 2A3. A 2A3 has a μ of only 4.2, so it needs about ±45 V on its grid for full output; a high-transconductance driver makes that swing from about a volt at its own grid, and its low plate resistance keeps the drive stiff. Move the supply, the bias and the plate resistor, and watch the gain, the swing and the plate limit.

Model: Child–Langmuir three-halves law fitted to the KB's μ and gm for the 6C45.

Heaters and warm-up

Nine tubes, two ways to heat a cathode. The 2A3s and the rectifier are directly heated: the filament is the cathode, so it emits within seconds. The 6C45s are indirectly heated: a heater warms a separate cathode sleeve, which takes tens of seconds. B+ arrives with the rectifier, before the drivers are awake.

    Datasheet: every filament and heater rating (KB), the amp's total draw (Woo's spec), the EML 5U4G-Mesh slow start (EML, via the KB) and the 5 to 10 minutes to operating temperature (Woo's quick-start guide). Typical, not measured: the other warm-up times and the shape of the B+ curve, including its small rise while the drivers are still cold.

    Rectifier sag

    A rectifier is a resistor that changes with current. Each curve's drop at rated current comes from the KB; below that it follows the three-halves law, and past the rating it turns dotted. More drop, more sag, softer dynamics. The EML 5U4G-Mesh is one current 5U4G; the Takatsuki TA-274B, which Woo lists for the WA33, is one 274B. Volts are at the filter input, before the choke.

      Output transformer

      Woo publishes no transformer data: no turns ratio, no primary impedance, no inductances. Every value on this card is an assumption you can move. Turns ratio squared sets the reflected load; primary inductance sets the bass corner; leakage inductance sets the treble corner. The one related figure Woo gives is the whole amp's response, −2 dB at 10 Hz and 60 kHz, marked ◇ on the plot. Change the headphones and the load line on the 2A3 curves above moves.
