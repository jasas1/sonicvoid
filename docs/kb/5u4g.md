# 5U4G Tube Family — WA33 Reference

## History

The 5U4G is a directly heated full-wave vacuum rectifier introduced in the United States around 1935, originally on an octal base (IO K8A). It descended from the earlier 5Z3 (UX4 base) and the ST-bottle 5U4; the "G" suffix denotes the glass ("GT" later) envelope shape. The RCA, Sylvania, Tung-Sol, GE, and Brimar datasheets all list it as a full-wave rectifier for power-supply duty. Radiomuseum.org classifies it as "Full-Wave Vacuum Rectifier, Octal (Int.Octal, IO) K8A, USA 1935." The European equivalent is the U52. The 5U4G was one of the most popular rectifiers in post-war American audio and radio gear, and it remains the stock rectifier in the Woo Audio WA33 headphone amplifier.

## Electrical specs

The following datasheet numbers are cited from the radiomuseum.org 5U4G entry and the Emission Labs (EML) 5U4G-Mesh data sheet.

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Filament voltage | 5 V (AC or DC) | radiomuseum, EML |
| Filament current | 3 A | radiomuseum, EML |
| Heating | Directly heated | radiomuseum |
| Max DC output current | 225 mA (capacitor input) | radiomuseum, EML |
| Max DC output current (choke input) | 265 mA | EML |
| Peak inverse voltage (PIV) | 1550 V | radiomuseum |
| Max AC per plate (transformer voltage) | 550 V | EML |
| Voltage drop at rated current | 44 V at 225 mA | radiomuseum |
| Max input capacitor (C1) | 40 µF | EML |
| AC peak current (repeatedly) | 800 mA | EML |
| Base | Octal (K8A), 5 pins used | radiomuseum, EML |

The EML data sheet notes that the tube follows Child-Langmuir law with a diode perveance K ≈ 0.000777. The EML charts are reproduced from the historical RCA data sheets and show derating: maximum current and maximum voltage cannot be used simultaneously.

## Construction variants

**5U4G** — Original ST/globe-to-straight-sided glass envelope, octal base, directly heated filament. The classic NOS type.

**5U4GA** — A later variant with a different envelope shape; listed by radiomuseum as "normally replaceable, slightly different." Uses a different glass shape but similar electrical ratings.

**5U4GB** — Electrically different from the 5U4G. EML states: "5U4GB is a version, with lower internal resistance and higher peak current is allowed. The GB version is not the same tube as the G Version." Replacing a 5U4G with a 5U4GB raises the rectified voltage; replacing a 5U4GB with a 5U4G can damage the rectifier. The 5U4GB also shares the octal base and 5 V / 3 A filament.

**5Z3** — The 5U4G and 5Z3 are electrically identical, differing only in socket: the 5U4G uses an octal socket (5 pins), the 5Z3 uses a 4-pin UX4 socket. EML: "5Z3-Mesh and 5U4G-Mesh, which are electrically identical, apart from the sockets."

**274B** — A Western Electric-design rectifier with a 5 V / 2 A filament (lower current than the 5U4G's 3 A). The 274B is the common WA33 rectifier upgrade and is not a direct 5U4G substitute — the filament winding must supply 2 A (not 3 A) and the amplifier must be designed for it. The WA33 is designed to accept both.

**5T4** — A related rectifier sometimes equated with the 5U4G, though with some spec differences noted in forum sources.

**Mullard GZ37** — An indirectly heated rectifier; not a 5U4G and not a drop-in substitute.

## Current production

**Emission Labs (EML)** — Produces the 5U4G-Mesh (and the electrically identical 5Z3-Mesh). Mesh-plate construction, octal socket (5 pins used), slow-start (2 s to first function, 7 s to full current). Filament 5 V / 3 A. EML also makes the 274B-Mesh as a separate product. EML tubes are larger than the historical NOS tubes.

**Sophia Electric** — Produces the 274B Aqua III, sold by Woo Audio as a WA33 rectifier upgrade.

**Psvane** — Produces the WE274B, sold by Woo Audio as a WA33 rectifier upgrade.

**Takatsuki** — Produces the TA-274B, sold by Woo Audio as a premium WA33 rectifier upgrade.

Other current-production 5U4G/5U4GB types from Russian and Chinese makers (Sovtek, Electro-Harmonix, Tung-Sol reissue, Shuguang, Linlai) are widely distributed but not verified from saved maker pages in this compilation.

## NOS

NOS (new old stock) 5U4G tubes were manufactured by RCA, Sylvania, Tung-Sol, GE, Brimar, and others. The Frank Philipse tube archive (frank.pocnet.net) lists 5U4G datasheets from Sylvania, Tung-Sol, and Brimar. Radiomuseum.org lists Brimar (STC) as a manufacturer and references GE 1973 "Essential Characteristics." NOS RCA 5U4G in ST or straight-sided glass are the most sought-after for audio use. NOS Sylvania 5U4G with brown base are sold by Woo Audio. The 5U4GB was produced by RCA, GE, and Tung-Sol. Svetlana (Winged-C) produced a 5U4G in Russia. The Mullard GZ37 is not a 5U4G.

## WA33 fit and warnings

The Woo Audio WA33 (2nd generation) Standard Edition is a fully-balanced vacuum-tube headphone amplifier using 4 × 6C45 driver tubes, 4 × 2A3 power tubes (matched quad), and 1 rectifier tube. The stock rectifier is the 5U4G. The common upgrade is the 274B (sold by Woo Audio as the Takatsuki TA-274B, Sophia Aqua III 274B, and Psvane WE274B). The WA33 has one rectifier socket.

Warnings:
- **5U4G vs 5U4GB**: Do not substitute a 5U4GB for a 5U4G without checking the circuit. The 5U4GB has lower internal resistance and produces higher rectified voltage; the 5U4G may be damaged if substituted for a 5U4GB.
- **Filament current**: The 5U4G draws 3 A at 5 V. The 274B draws 2 A at 5 V. The WA33 power transformer must be rated for the correct filament current; the amp is designed to accept both.
- **Input capacitor limit**: The first capacitor after the rectifier must not exceed 40 µF; exceeding this causes high charge-current peaks, tube damage, and hum radiation.
- **Minimum transformer resistance**: For capacitor-input filter, the transformer winding resistance must not be below ~170–230 Ω; otherwise add series resistors.
- **Slow-start**: NOS and EML 5U4G are slow-start tubes; the filament takes seconds to reach full emission, which protects the amplifier but means B+ rises gradually.
- **Counterfeit mesh**: Some sellers offer "mesh-plate" 5U4G that are not genuine mesh; buy from reputable dealers.
- **Arcing**: A 5U4G operated beyond its PIV (1550 V) or with excessive input capacitance can arc internally, visible as a white spark at switch-on.
- **300B does not fit**: The WA33 uses 2A3 power tubes and a 5U4G/274B rectifier; 300B tubes do not fit this amplifier.

## Sonic lore

The EML 5U4G-Mesh is described by its maker as a slow-start tube that protects the amplifier and enables "ultra low ripple" when used with a choke-input filter. Choke-loaded operation is recommended for lowest hum-field radiation and best tube life. In the WA33 community, the 274B upgrade (Takatsuki, Sophia, Psvane) is commonly reported to produce a more refined, "black background" presentation than the stock 5U4G, with the EML mesh and NOS RCA 5U4G valued for a warm, smooth character. Forum sources (head-fi.org, diyaudio.com) were not accessible as text during this compilation (JavaScript-rendered or login-walled), so sonic descriptors are drawn from maker pages and the Woo Audio product listings rather than direct forum quotes.
