# 274B Tube Family — Reference Entry

## History

The 274B is a directly heated, full-wave vacuum rectifier originally developed by Western Electric in the 1930s. It was designed to complement the WE 300B in the WE91 cinema amplifier, which produced 5.6 W for movie-theatre sound reproduction. The 274A (UX4 4-pin base, 1936) and 274B (octal base) are electrically identical; only the socket differs. The octal-base 274B was intended to fit the same socket as the industry-standard RCA 5U4G, but its strict 4 µF first-capacitor limit prevented broad adoption — most equipment designed for the 5U4G used up to 47 µF. Japanese audiophiles rediscovered the WE 274B and 300B in the mid-1980s, and by the mid-1990s NOS WE 274B tubes commanded stratospheric prices. Today a single untested vintage WE 274B may be listed at $4,999 in auction. Emission Labs was the first company to re-introduce the 274 tube, in 2001. Sophia Electric debuted the Princess 274B rigid plate the same year, followed by a mesh-plate version in 2002 and the blue-glass Aqua line in 2016.

Typical capacitor-input operating point (EML): DC output 450 V, total rectified current 140 mA, first capacitor 4 µF, choke 10 H, second capacitor max 20 µF. The 274B has higher source impedance than the 5U4G, which EML describes as producing "improved sound compared to lower impedance rectifier tubes such as 5U4G." For comparison, the 5U4G runs a 3 A filament, allows 40 µF first capacitor, and delivers 225 mA (cap input) / 265 mA (choke input).

## Construction variants

- **274A**: UX4 (4-pin) base, electrically identical to 274B. Original WE type dated 1936. Will NOT fit an octal 5U4G/274B socket.
- **274B**: Octal base, the standard replacement for 5U4G. The type used in the Woo Audio WA33.
- **Mesh plate**: Some modern reissues (EML, Sophia Princess) use a mesh anode for aesthetic and sonic reasons; mesh-plate variants typically have stricter current and capacitor limits (Sophia mesh: 8 µF max first cap).
- **Solid plate**: The original WE 274B used a long solid plate; Elrog ER274B and Sophia rigid-plate use solid-plate construction.
- **Blue glass**: Sophia Electric's Aqua line uses blue-tinted glass bottles, a cosmetic signature of the brand.

## Current production

**Emission Labs (EML)** — Czech Republic. EML 274B mesh. Re-introduced the 274 in 2001. Hard-metal construction, gold-plated black ceramic socket, hand-blown glass. 150 mA cap input / 200 mA choke input; 4 µF first cap at high voltage, 20 µF at low voltage.

**Sophia Electric** — USA. Aqua III 274B (current, blue glass, $299–350), Aqua II 274B (max 220 mA, 2.8 A filament, lower impedance than any prior 274B, but had compatibility issues with some DACs and amps), Princess 274B mesh plate ($175, clearance), 274A mesh ($250–300). The Aqua line lifted the 4 µF capacitor limitation, broadening 274B applicability.

**Elrog (Deutsche Elektronenröhren Manufaktur GmbH)** — Germany. ER274B. Solid plate, octal base. PIV 1,500 V, 225 mA choke input / 160 mA cap input, peak current per plate 675 mA. Has different maximum ratings from the original 274B (higher current capacity at the same PIV).

**Psvane** — China. Art 274B, 274B-TII, Treasure 274B-Z. Multiple variants across product lines; product pages are image-based with limited published text specs.

**Not verified**: Takatsuki TA-274B (website DNS failure), Linlai (connection timeout), KR Audio (no 274B listed on minimal website), Western Electric reissue (westernelectric.com lists only the 300B; no 274B reissue offered as of 2026-10-12).

## NOS

**Western Electric 274B** (USA, 1930s–1940s) — the original. Octal base, directly heated, full-wave rectifier. Max 150 mA cap input, 4 µF first capacitor. Launched to complement the WE 300B in WE91 cinema amplifiers. NOS tubes are on the "endangered species" list and command $1,500–5,000+ depending on condition and testing.

**Western Electric 274A** (USA, 1936) — UX4 (4-pin) base version, electrically equivalent to the 274B. The original WE datasheet dates from 1936.

## WA33 fit and warnings

The Woo Audio WA33 (2nd generation) Standard Edition is a fully balanced, push-pull Class-A headphone amplifier using 4 × 6C45 driver tubes, 4 × 2A3 power tubes, and 1 rectifier tube. The product description names the 274B rectifier, but the default-tube specification lists the 5U4G. The 274B is the common upgrade and fits the same octal socket.

Key fit considerations:
- **Socket**: 1 octal rectifier socket. 274B and 5U4G both fit. 274A (UX4) does NOT fit. 300B does NOT fit this amplifier.
- **First capacitor**: The 274B allows only 4 µF (WE) to 8 µF (Sophia rigid/mesh) at the first capacitor position. If the WA33 power supply was designed for the 5U4G (which permits 40–47 µF), installing a NOS WE 274B without verifying the first capacitor value risks arcing. The Sophia Aqua line was specifically designed to lift this limitation.
- **Current**: The 274B is rated at 150 mA cap input vs the 5U4G's 225 mA. The WA33 must draw within the 274B's rating.
- **Filament**: The 274B draws 2 A at 5 V; the 5U4G draws 3 A. A transformer winding rated for the 5U4G can power a 274B, but not vice versa.

Warnings:
1. **Arcing**: The WE 274B long plate sparks when the first capacitor exceeds ~4–8 µF, which can blow fuses or damage the tube.
2. **Counterfeits**: NOS WE 274B tubes at $1,500–5,000 attract relabels and counterfeits.
3. **Compatibility**: Sophia Aqua II 274B may cause premature failure in some amplifiers and DACs (Decware SE84UFO, some Lampizator units); the Aqua III is the safer choice.
4. **Mesh plate limits**: Mesh-plate 274B variants have stricter current and capacitor limits than solid-plate types.

## Sonic lore

**NOS Western Electric 274B**: Jeff Day (Positive Feedback) writes: "Of all the rectifiers I've tried over the decades, the 274B rectifier is my favorite by leaps and bounds." He describes "lit-from-within holographic display of imaging and soundstage cues," "natural tonality," and "spectacular sound-staging abilities." The 274B is said to deliver "a wider and deeper soundstage, and deliver the musical magic that is otherwise missing when not using a 274B rectifier."

**Sophia Electric Aqua II 274B**: Day's 2023 review: "a beautifully natural tonal balance, a huge sense of recorded acoustic, gorgeous tone color, a vivid sense of imaging presence, excellent dynamics, and increased clarity and resolution of nuance." He calls it "another home run" and "an easy recommendation." Won the 2023 Positive Feedback Writers' Choice Award. The Aqua II has "higher resolution — akin to watching 8K video instead of 1080."

**Sophia Electric Aqua III 274B**: Sophia Electric describes it as offering "nearly identical sonic performance to the Mark II" while being "virtually foolproof for a wide range of applications." Recommended over the Aqua II for 300B amplifiers, Decware amplifiers, and Lampizator DACs.

**EML 274B mesh**: EML describes the 274B as producing "improved sound compared to lower impedance rectifier tubes such as 5U4G, or even solid state rectifiers," with "less mechanical hum of the transformer" and "less electrical and magnetic hum radiation into the preamplifier."
